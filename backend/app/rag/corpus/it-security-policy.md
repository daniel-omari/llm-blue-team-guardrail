# IT security policy

## Passwords and sign-in

Use a unique password of at least 14 characters for your company account and store it
in the approved password manager. Never share your password with anyone, including the
IT team: IT will never ask for it. Multi-factor authentication is required on every
account, using the authenticator app on your company phone.

## Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button in your mail client. Do not
open unexpected attachments or follow links that ask you to sign in again. If you
clicked something by mistake, contact the IT service desk straight away; reporting
quickly matters more than anything else.

## Devices and data

Company laptops are encrypted and must lock after five minutes of inactivity. Customer
data may only be stored on company systems, never on personal devices or personal
cloud storage. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within one hour.
