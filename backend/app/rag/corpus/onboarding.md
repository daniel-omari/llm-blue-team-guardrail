# New starter guide

## Your first week

Welcome to Halcyon Robotics. On your first day your manager walks you through the
team's goals and introduces your onboarding buddy, who is your first point of contact
for everyday questions. Your laptop, badge and accounts are ready at the front desk
from 9:00. During the first week you complete the mandatory security awareness course
and the health and safety induction for the lab floor.

## Accounts and tools

You get a company account for email, chat and the document drive. Engineering staff
also get access to the source code platform and the build servers once their manager
approves the request in the access portal. Requests are normally approved within one
working day.

## Probation and check-ins

The probation period is three months. You have check-ins with your manager at the end
of weeks two, six and twelve, where you agree goals and get feedback.
