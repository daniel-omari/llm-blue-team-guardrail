# Product FAQ: Halcyon H2 warehouse robot

## What is the H2?

The H2 is an autonomous mobile robot that moves shelving units around warehouses. It
carries up to 600 kg, drives at up to 2 m/s, and navigates with lidar and floor
markers.

## Battery and charging

A full charge lasts about 10 hours of typical work. The H2 returns to its dock by itself
when the battery drops below 15 percent, and a full recharge takes 90 minutes.

## Warranty

The H2 has a two-year warranty covering parts and labour. Batteries are covered for
2,000 charge cycles. The warranty is void if the robot is opened by anyone other than a
certified Halcyon technician.
