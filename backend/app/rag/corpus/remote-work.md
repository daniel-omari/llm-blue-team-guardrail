# Hybrid and remote work

## Office days

Teams work in a hybrid pattern: at least two days a week in the office, agreed with
your manager. Lab and hardware teams usually need more office time because of the
equipment.

## Working from home

Connect through the company VPN whenever you work outside the office. Use a private
space for calls that involve customer or personnel information. You can borrow a
monitor and chair from the office for your home setup through the facilities desk.

## Working abroad

Working from another country for up to 20 days a year is allowed with manager approval
and an HR check, because of tax and insurance rules.
