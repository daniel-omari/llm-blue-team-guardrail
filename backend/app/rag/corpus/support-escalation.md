# Customer support escalation

## Support tiers

Tier 1 handles customer contact and known issues using the troubleshooting guides.
Tier 2 is the product specialist team, who take issues that need log analysis or remote
diagnostics. Tier 3 is engineering, for suspected defects and safety issues.

## Response times

Priority 1 (a fleet is down or a safety issue): response within 1 hour, around the
clock. Priority 2 (degraded operation): within 4 business hours. Priority 3 (questions
and minor issues): within 2 business days.

## Safety issues

Any report of a robot hitting a person or behaving unpredictably near people is
escalated straight to Tier 3 and the safety officer, whatever its priority.
