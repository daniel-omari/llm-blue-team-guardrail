# Travel and expenses policy

## Booking travel

Book trains and flights through the company travel portal at least two weeks ahead
where possible. Economy class is standard; business class needs director approval for
flights over six hours.

## Daily allowances

The meal allowance is 45 EUR per day when travelling for work, and hotels are capped
at 150 EUR per night (220 EUR in capital cities). Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Claiming expenses

Submit claims in the expenses app within 30 days, with an itemised receipt for every
item over 10 EUR. Claims without receipts are rejected. Reimbursements are paid with
the next monthly salary.
